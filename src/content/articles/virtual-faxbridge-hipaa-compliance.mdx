In industries like healthcare, legal practice, insurance, and municipal government, faxing remains a non-negotiable legal and regulatory standard.

However, traditional faxing introduces two major problems:

1. **Expensive Dedicated Analog Lines**: Phone companies charge $80 to $150 per month just to maintain a single analog POTS line connected to a fax machine.
2. **VoIP Incompatibility**: Standard VoIP phone lines use audio compression algorithms that frequently corrupt analog fax tones, resulting in failed transmissions and garbled pages.

## What is FaxBridge?

**FaxBridge** is Net Express's hardware-plus-cloud solution that internet-enables your existing physical multi-function printer or fax machine.

Instead of transmitting audio over an unreliable VoIP connection, the compact FaxBridge device encrypts the document and transmits it directly to our high-security cloud fax servers via HTTPS (TLS 1.3 encryption).

## Key Advantages of FaxBridge

### 1. Keep Your Existing Fax Machine
Staff don't have to change their workflow. They walk up to the machine, load paper, enter the recipient's fax number, and press Start — exactly as they always have.

### 2. Dual Inbound & Outbound Capabilities
Receive faxes on paper from your machine, or have incoming faxes automatically converted into encrypted PDFs sent directly to designated email inboxes or secure cloud folders.

### 3. Strict HIPAA & Regulatory Compliance
FaxBridge meets all federal HIPAA security standards:
- End-to-end 256-bit AES encryption in transit and at rest
- Automatic transmission audit logs with timestamped delivery receipts
- Elimination of unattended paper faxes sitting exposed in public office trays

### 4. Significant Monthly Cost Reduction
Eliminate $100+/mo analog phone line surcharges. FaxBridge operates over your existing internet connection for just a fraction of the cost ($15/mo per active line).
