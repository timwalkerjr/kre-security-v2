## What Integrated Phone and Internet Means

Integrated phone and internet service combines your voice communications and data connectivity through a unified network infrastructure. Instead of maintaining separate analog phone lines and internet connections, both services operate over the same high-capacity data connection.

This integration is made possible through Voice over IP (VoIP) technology, which converts voice calls into digital data packets that travel over your internet connection. The result is a single, simplified network that handles all your communication needs.

### Key Components of Integration

A properly integrated solution includes several essential elements working together:

- **Dedicated data connection:** A reliable, high-capacity internet connection serves as the foundation for both voice and data traffic.
- **[SIP trunking or hosted VoIP](/#services):** Technology that enables phone calls to travel over your data connection.
- **Quality of Service (QoS) management:** Network configuration that prioritizes voice traffic to ensure call quality.
- **Compatible phone equipment:** Either your existing PBX system or [hosted VoIP](/#services) phones that connect to the network.

## The Business Benefits of Integration

Combining your phone and internet services delivers measurable advantages across cost, performance, and operational flexibility.

### Significant Cost Savings

Integrated solutions eliminate the need for dedicated voice connections at each location, providing substantial cost reductions. Traditional analog phone lines carry recurring monthly costs that multiply across multiple locations. With SIP trunking, all traffic from multiple locations connects into a central site, eliminating redundant voice connections.

You also avoid unnecessary hardware investments. There's no need to incur additional hardware costs when you can use your existing PBX such as Avaya, NEC, Lucent, Nortel, Meridian or Mitel. SIP trunking is the ideal way to use your existing PBX or key system to take advantage of the latest Voice over IP technology.

### Highest Quality Voice Traffic

We provide a dedicated path for voice traffic using the latest in broadband technology. By segregating voice traffic on its own connection, your calls are always of the highest quality. This dedicated approach prevents data-heavy applications from interfering with call clarity.

Quality matters in business communications. Integrated solutions deliver consistent, reliable voice quality that matches or exceeds traditional phone lines when properly implemented.

### Scalability That Grows With Your Business

Integrated phone and internet solutions scale easily as your business needs change. Adding new phone lines doesn't require scheduling telephone company installations or waiting for analog line provisioning. With SIP trunking or hosted VoIP, you can add capacity through simple configuration changes.

Internet bandwidth scales similarly. Speeds are available up to 150M on coax and 1G on fiber. By providing a cable partner data pipe, you will never outgrow your connection. This scalability ensures your communications infrastructure adapts to business growth without costly infrastructure overhauls.

### Multi-Location Connectivity Without Expensive Hardware

Want to connect multiple locations with extension dialing? Integrated solutions make this possible without expensive hardware investments. Employees at different sites can reach each other by dialing extensions, creating a unified communications environment across your entire organization.

This capability is particularly valuable for businesses operating multiple retail locations, branch offices, or distributed facilities. It creates communication efficiencies that were previously available only through costly private network infrastructure.

## Understanding Your Integration Options

Different businesses require different approaches to integration. The right solution depends on your existing equipment, number of locations, and specific communication needs.

### SIP Trunking: Leverage Your Existing PBX

SIP trunking connects your existing phone system to Voice over IP technology without replacing your current equipment. It's easy to simplify your network by using a private data connection to pass your voice traffic. You can leverage VoIP technology with no need to replace functional phone systems.

This approach works with most business phone systems. Because of our established relationships with a wide array of hardware vendors and service providers, we can furnish our clients with the most dynamic SIP trunking solutions available. This compatibility means you protect your existing technology investment while gaining the benefits of modern voice services.

### Hosted VoIP: Eliminate Hardware Management

Hosted VoIP is a form of business VoIP services where the VoIP equipment, servers and services are hosted by the VoIP provider, which manages calls and routes them to and from the subscriber's existing telephony system and equipment. Hosted VoIP can save companies from the expensive costs of having to invest in VoIP equipment and manage the system.

With hosted solutions, your phones connect directly to cloud-based systems. There's no on-premise PBX to maintain, upgrade, or repair. This approach reduces capital expenses and shifts the technical management responsibility to your service provider.

### Fiber Optic Connectivity: Maximum Performance

Fiber optic cable is the highest capacity and fastest communications medium available. Operating at the speed of light, fiber conveys voice and data communications with exceptional reliability and minimal latency. For businesses with high bandwidth requirements or multiple locations, fiber connectivity provides the foundation for optimal integrated services.

## Making Integration Work for Your Business

Successful integration requires proper planning and implementation. Several factors influence how well integrated phone and internet services perform for your specific situation.

### Network Design Considerations

The architecture of your data network directly impacts voice quality and reliability. Proper network design includes adequate bandwidth allocation, quality of service configuration, and network segmentation that prioritizes voice traffic during periods of heavy data usage.

A dedicated path for voice traffic ensures consistent performance. This segregation prevents large file transfers, video streaming, or other bandwidth-intensive activities from degrading call quality.

### Internet Connection Requirements

Your internet connection must provide sufficient bandwidth and reliability for combined voice and data traffic. Connection requirements vary based on the number of simultaneous calls, data usage patterns, and application needs.

Businesses should also consider connection redundancy. A backup internet connection ensures communication continuity if the primary connection experiences issues. We can provide solutions using the latest in broadband technology including 5G wireless as backup connectivity.

### Equipment Compatibility

Most modern business phone systems support SIP trunking and can integrate with VoIP services. However, very old analog systems may require adapters or replacement to work with integrated solutions. We evaluate your existing equipment and recommend the most cost-effective integration approach.

For businesses with existing fax machines, [FaxBridge](/#services) devices provide simple integration. Our FaxBridge is a plug-and-play analog telephone adapter that connects your fax machine to your internet service. No complex setup or installation is required. FaxBridges ship preconfigured and already assigned to your account. This eliminates the need for POTS lines and requires only an internet connection and power source.

### Unified Communications Features

Integrated phone and internet services support unified communications capabilities including voicemail-to-email, call forwarding to mobile devices, presence management, and integrated messaging. These features create communication flexibility that improves responsiveness and productivity.
